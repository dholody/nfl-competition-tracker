Unique Opponents by Team Segment width = that team's actual win probability in the game
"Unique" opponents are identified empirically from the schedule data — any opponent not played by all four division teams — rather than from the NFL's scheduling formula directly, so this reflects this year's actual assignments including any 17th-game or standings-based games.
Unique Opponents by Team above uses each team's own actual win probability (their FPI vs. the opponent's). The Schedule Advantage chart uses a different, opponent-only metric: it sums a hypothetical league-average team's (FPI = 0) modeled win probability across each team's unique games to get an expected win total from just those games, then compares that to the division average — isolating how hard the opponents are, independent of how good the team facing them happens to be. The modeled win probability comes from treating FPI as a normal distribution over score margin (σ ≈ 13.5 points), a standard NFL approximation, not an ESPN-reported figure.
